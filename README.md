# Malaysia Help Numbers

A community-compiled quick-reference of Malaysia's emergency lines, agency hotlines, and public help services — organised by urgency.

Available in **English**, **Bahasa Melayu**, and **中文** (Simplified Chinese).

**[→ Download from the project page](https://xnguo.github.io/malaysia-help-numbers/)**

---

## What's in it

Four tiers of contacts, each with what to expect when you call, what to have ready, and any catch:

- **Tier 1 — Life-threatening** · 999, 112, cardiac (IJN), hospital A&E direct lines, active fraud (NSRC 997 + bank fraud hotlines)
- **Tier 2 — Urgent** · poison, snakebite, gas leak, mental health crisis, domestic violence, missing child, highway breakdown, cyber incident, disaster
- **Tier 3 — Important (office hours)** · banking disputes, consumer complaints, employment issues, drug treatment, blood donation
- **Tier 4 — Public services & complaints** · traffic violations, corruption, education, tax, environment, animal welfare, human rights
- **Visitor section** · tourist police, lost passport, OSCC sexual assault response, English-language mental health support

Every number is tagged with cost (free / local-rate / normal-rate / SMS-rate) and is tap-to-call on a phone.

## What it is, what it isn't

- **Compiled with AI assistance** from public agency sources (each agency's own website)
- **Every number verified** against the agency's own site at the date of issue
- **Not an official publication** of any agency
- **Not affiliated** with the Government of Malaysia or any listed organisation
- **No guarantee** of accuracy or fitness for purpose — verify against the agency's own site before relying on any specific detail
- **Hotlines change** — if a number doesn't connect in a real emergency, call 999 and the operators will route you

## Reporting errors

If you spot an outdated number or other error, please open an [issue](https://github.com/xnguo/malaysia-help-numbers/issues) with:

- Which language version (EN / BM / CN / EN-BM-CN)
- Which page or tier
- What's wrong
- A link to the agency's current official source if you have one

Confirmed errors will be fixed in the next version.
